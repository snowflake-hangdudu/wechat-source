## UKIYOE · 参考图定制浮世绘

此为本次参考图的个人适配方向，不是新增 GitHub 编号。重点是旧纸、墨线、低饱和分色、印刷磨损与平面层次；不将参考图服装设为必须条件。

```text
Create ONE finished standalone vertical 9:16 illustration featuring one clearly ADULT [CHARACTER_NAME] from [SERIES].
Identity lock: [IDENTITY_LOCK]. Preserve recognizable facial structure, hair, eyes and signature markings.
Scene: [SCENE]. Action: [ACTION]. Wardrobe: [WARDROBE]. Expression: [EXPRESSION]. Camera and framing: [FRAMING]. Palette: [PALETTE].
Style: vintage Japanese woodblock-inspired character illustration, matched to the supplied visual reference. Use warm aged ochre washi, visible paper fibers, irregular mottling and restrained surface wear. Define the face, hair and clothing with precise dark keyblock-like contours, fine tapered interior lines and sparse directional hatching. Build form from clearly separated muted moss-green, deep indigo, dusty rose, ochre and cream pigment areas, with subtle uneven ink density and gently abraded print texture.
Translate the character's recognizable face into this printed visual language while retaining a mature, natural appearance. Use planar depth and economical stylized environmental shapes. When appropriate to the approved scene, place rhythmic waves, curling cloud outlines, foliage or scattered petals near the edges; these motifs are optional and must not replace the requested setting. Keep the face and silhouette dominant and surrounding detail quieter.
Use the attached reference for medium, contour quality, pigment behavior, paper aging and color relationships only. Do not automatically copy its crossed arms, outfit, cherry blossoms, waves or exact composition. This style does not require a kimono.
Maintain anatomically balanced adult proportions, believable joints, stable weight and coherent hand-to-object contact. Clothing is tasteful and securely arranged.
No photorealism, glossy 3D surfaces, neon lighting, smooth airbrushed gradients, generic watercolor filtering, excessive distress that obscures the face, extra limbs, text, logos or watermark. Deliver one coherent image, not a contact sheet.
```
