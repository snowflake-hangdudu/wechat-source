## 1. FD-026 Personal Edition（主画风）

原库 FD-026 的个人调整版，以参考原图为画风锚点，不能只使用编号。

参考：[舷窗旁的娜美](../个人主题图片生成/FD-026-舷窗旁的娜美.png)、[线稿／读信](../个人主题图片生成/FD-026-线稿.png)。

**Positive prompt｜正向画风**

```text
Delicate emotional female pencil-and-ink line art, extremely fine expressive flowing hair strands and delicate facial marks, large airy creamy-paper negative space, diluted soft transparent watercolor and colored-pencil washes with natural paper grain. Poised unforced expression, relaxed body, elegant everyday fashion silhouette. Mature natural beauty with anatomically harmonious feminine proportions. Keep backgrounds sparse and prioritize emotional character drawing, with clear clothing silhouettes and believable fabric drape.
```

**Style constraints｜画风限制**（另加文末共用限制）

```text
Avoid hyper-detailed generic anime CG, glossy plastic skin, heavy 3D rendering, thick uniform outlines, opaque heavy paint, dense fully rendered backgrounds, exaggerated body proportions and a fixed stock smile. Do not copy the reference image's outfit, pose or setting.
```

## 4. 和服主题

和服是服饰主题，每张只选一种表现方法。目前缺少独立和服参考原图；下面沿用既有专场规范，不表示已核对成品效果。

**Positive prompt｜正向画风**

从下方选一个模块，再加服饰要求；不要将三个模块一起粘贴。

- **日本画**：
  `Classical Nihonga mineral-pigment painting on silk or washi, restrained elegant colors, refined garment contours, delicate decorative patterns and quiet open space.`
- **大正浪漫**：
  `Vintage Taisho Roman fashion illustration, elegant clear linework, softly faded print texture, restrained nostalgic colors and period-consistent kimono or kimono with hakama.`
- **京都淡彩**：
  `Delicate Kyoto watercolor on textured washi, fine light contours, transparent pale washes, soft paper grain, restrained garment patterns and generous airy negative space.`

服饰要求：

```text
Preserve the adult character's recognizable appearance and mature presence. A culturally coherent, fully wrapped kimono with the wearer's left collar over the right, a correctly placed obi, consistent sleeve construction and appropriate accessories. Graceful natural posture and believable fabric folds. Clothing and setting follow the current written theme.
```

**Style constraints｜画风限制**（另加文末共用限制）

```text
Do not mix all three rendering methods. Avoid reversed kimono collars, incoherent obi construction, impossible sleeves, random costume accessories, gratuitously exposed clothing and generic glossy anime CG. Do not substitute the ukiyo-e reference as an exact reference for every kimono rendering method.
```

## 5. 浮世绘主题（个人融合版）

**Positive prompt｜正向画风**

```text
A modern character illustration fused with ukiyo-e woodblock decorative language. Preserve the adult character's recognizable face and identity. Clear ink keylines, restrained indigo, earthy vermilion and warm cream color separations, flat graphic shapes, visible washi fibers and subtle worn print texture. Rhythmic decorative forms and stylized environmental shapes. All subjects and motifs follow the current written theme rather than the reference image's contents.
```

**Style constraints｜画风限制**（另加文末共用限制）

```text
Avoid photorealistic lighting, airbrushed gradients, glossy 3D materials and generic digital anime painting with a paper filter. Do not automatically add waves, cherry blossoms, a red sun or ships from the reference. Preserve character identity rather than replacing the face with a generic historical beauty.
```

## 共用英文模块

**人物与画面要求**：每次补入角色身份和本张内容。

```text
Create ONE finished standalone vertical 2:3 wallpaper illustration.
Subject: unmistakably ADULT [CHARACTER NAME] from [SERIES].
Character identity: [CANONICAL HAIR, EYES, FACE, ACCESSORIES AND PERSONALITY].
Clothing: [WARDROBE]. Pose/action: [ACTION]. Setting: [SCENE].
Mood: [EMOTION]. Color palette: [COLORS]. Framing: [SHOT TYPE].
An adult feminine silhouette with naturally full but anatomically harmonious bust-waist-hip proportions, balanced with shoulder width and height. Refined mature beauty, tasteful coverage, believable joints, natural center of gravity and supported body weight.
Use the attached image only as a style reference for linework, brushwork, medium, paper texture and color tendencies. The written theme defines character identity and all picture content; create a new outfit, pose, setting and composition as specified.
```

**Common constraints｜共用限制词**

```text
No underage or childlike depiction, wrong character identity, exaggerated breasts or waist, impossible anatomy, twisted joints, unsupported poses, extra or missing limbs, extra or fused fingers, gratuitous nudity, see-through intimate areas or sexualized framing. No letters, titles, numbering, logos, watermark, collage panels or decorative frames. No unintended cuts through essential joints or body parts; preserve the native aspect ratio without subsequent cropping or stretching.
```

调用顺序：**人物与画面要求 + 一种正向画风模块 + 对应画风限制 + 共用限制**，同时提供对应原图。替换所有方括号占位符；和服须先选择单一子方向。

若生图工具有独立 Negative Prompt 栏，将两组限制放入该栏；若没有，直接作为英文约束附在主提示词后。限制词和语言本身不保证结果，仍需参考原图并检查实际成图。

新增画风继续沿用：**名称、参考图、英文正向提示词、英文限制词**。
