# 死神 BLEACH｜江户浮世绘系列英文提示词

> 8 位角色 · 独立 9:16 竖屏壁纸 · English Prompt Collection

## 使用方法

使用 **Master Prompt + 对应角色 Character Prompt + Negative Prompt** 的组合方式。若上传最初的《火影忍者》江户版画图片，只作为 **style reference**，不作为需要改动的底图；要求一张图仅一名角色。单独生成不同角色，以便得到可分别保存的壁纸。

**系列视觉重点：**尸魂界、斩魄刀、死霸装、破面与海浪；人物服装与武器辨识度优先。

## 一、通用英文母提示词（Master Prompt）

```text
A masterpiece independent 9:16 vertical BLEACH female-character wallpaper rendered as an authentic traditional Japanese Edo-period ukiyo-e mokuhanga woodblock print fused with precise and graceful 2D anime character design.

Warm antique yellowed washi paper with worn visible fibers and cracked distressed matte ink pigments, thin hand-engraved black outlines, intricate kimono textile patterns, excellent delicate facial detail, believable graceful hands and elegant windswept sleeves. A richly composed three-quarter portrait with exactly one clearly identifiable BLEACH heroine as the central focal point.

Frame the figure with flowering pink sakura or plum branches, floating petals, spectacular curling Hokusai-inspired dark-indigo foaming ocean waves near the lower edges, highly ornate engraved Japanese cloud swirls, old mountain landscapes and a faded large brick-red or vermilion sun. Add subtle Soul Society architecture, zanpakuto patterns, ice, stealth, desert ruins or shark/sea motifs to express that specific character.

Restrained antiqued colors of parchment beige, dusty peach, terracotta, navy, sage, faded crimson and antique gold while preserving essential hair color, skin tone, distinctive original shihakusho or Arrancar costume and weapon forms. Exquisite full-bleed collectible wall-scroll portrait, one character only.
```

## 四、出图一致性规范

- 建议画幅：**9:16**，高清竖屏壁纸；每张独立单人画面。
- 视觉母题：和纸旧纹、木版印刷颗粒、樱花／梅花、靛蓝海浪、朱红日轮与卷云。
- 角色保真：发型、瞳色、服装、武器或能力元素优先准确，浮世绘主要体现在绘制语言和背景纹样。
- 使用组合：`Master Prompt → Character Prompt → Negative Prompt`。
- 注：根据本次已生成的系列画面与英文提示词整理；不同图像模型可能产生不同细节。
