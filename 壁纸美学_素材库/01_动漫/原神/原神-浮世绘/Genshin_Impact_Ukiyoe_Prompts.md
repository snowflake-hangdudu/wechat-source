# 原神 · Genshin Impact｜江户浮世绘系列英文提示词

> 8 位角色 · 独立 9:16 竖屏壁纸 · English Prompt Collection

## 使用方法

使用 **Master Prompt + 对应角色 Character Prompt + Negative Prompt** 的组合方式。若上传最初的《火影忍者》江户版画图片，只作为 **style reference**，不作为需要改动的底图；要求一张图仅一名角色。单独生成不同角色，以便得到可分别保存的壁纸。

**系列视觉重点：**严格按照用户选定的 S 级八人名单：芙宁娜、雷电将军、八重神子、神里绫华、胡桃、甘雨、纳西妲、玛薇卡。

## 一、通用英文母提示词（Master Prompt）

```text
A masterpiece 9:16 vertical wallpaper featuring exactly one faithful, instantly recognizable Genshin Impact female character in a sophisticated fusion of traditional Japanese Edo-period ukiyo-e mokuhanga woodblock print and extremely refined two-dimensional anime portraiture.

Rich antique golden-beige washi paper with visible worn fibers, distressed matte mineral pigments, exquisite engraved black ink lines, intricate textile patterns, graceful flowing hair and expressive anime eyes. A balanced, elegant central three-quarter portrait with finely drawn hands and canonical signature costume silhouettes.

Frame the scene with lush flowering cherry/plum branches, drifting pink petals, deep-indigo foamy Hokusai-inspired Japanese sea waves, traditional engraved spiral clouds, distant atmospheric mountains and a large faded vermilion sun. Give each character her unique elemental features through restrained historic printmaking effects: Hydro water ribbons, Electro cloud patterns, Cryo snowflakes, Dendro leaves or Pyro flames.

A coherent vintage palette of parchment, terracotta, dusty rose, indigo navy, muted sage and antique gold while preserving each character's distinguishing original hair, eyes, clothes, weapons and accessories. Elegant full-bleed handcrafted collectible fine art print; one character only.
```

## 三、通用负面提示词（Negative Prompt）

```text
No typography, no kanji, no letter-like symbols, no logo, no watermark, no signature, no frame, no collage, no multi-panel composition, no additional people, no Naruto headbands, no photorealistic image, no CGI or 3D, no shiny digital gradients, no neon lighting, no distorted hands, no incorrect costume, no inaccurate anatomy, no exaggerated fanservice or sexualization.
```

## 四、出图一致性规范

- 建议画幅：**9:16**，高清竖屏壁纸；每张独立单人画面。
- 视觉母题：和纸旧纹、木版印刷颗粒、樱花／梅花、靛蓝海浪、朱红日轮与卷云。
- 角色保真：发型、瞳色、服装、武器或能力元素优先准确，浮世绘主要体现在绘制语言和背景纹样。
- 使用组合：`Master Prompt → Character Prompt → Negative Prompt`。
- 注：根据本次已生成的系列画面与英文提示词整理；不同图像模型可能产生不同细节。
